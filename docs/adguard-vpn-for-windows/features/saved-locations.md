![Saved locations *border](https://cdn.adguardvpn.com/content/kb/vpn/windows/saved_locations.png)

You can save your favorite locations to access them quickly. Just hover over a location, click the bookmark icon that appears, and it will be saved. Your marked locations will then show up in the *Saved* tab.
