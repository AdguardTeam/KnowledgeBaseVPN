AdGuard VPN for Windows:

- Protects from network traffic interception (spoofing). AdGuard VPN creates an encrypted tunnel between your device and a remote server. All your Internet traffic passes through this tunnel, so your data is protected along the way. And thanks to [TrustTunnel, our unique protocol](https://trusttunnel.org/), you’re guaranteed a fast and secure connection.

- Masks your IP address. Your true IP address is the key to your personal data for cybercriminals. Your name, email address, phone number, credit card information can all fall into the hands of fraudsters if you don’t hide your IP. With AdGuard VPN, all your traffic goes through an encrypted tunnel and comes to the VPN server. The web server registers the IP address of the endpoint of the tunnel, i.e. the VPN server, and not the device’s real IP address.

- Hides your real location. What does this give you? For example, the ability to book a hotel at locals’ rates or hide from geotargeted advertising.

AdGuard VPN for Windows has many advantages, so we’ve dedicated [a separate article](/general/why-adguard-vpn) to them. Here, however, we want to focus more on the application itself and how it works.

## Starting with the app

To start using AdGuard VPN for Windows, download the app from [our website](https://adguard-vpn.com/welcome.html), install it, and sign in. The installer will ask you to accept the User Agreement and the Privacy policy, and to decide whether AdGuard can collect anonymized app usage data.

![Home screen](https://cdn.adguardvpn.com/content/kb/vpn/windows/saved_locations.png)

On the **Home** screen, you can see the AdGuard VPN *Connect/Disconnect* button and the selected server. On the right side of the screen there is a list of available locations. The fastest location, i.e. the one with the lowest ping, is shown at the top of the list. Locations you use often can be saved to the *Saved* tab.

At the top of the screen there is a navigation panel with five tabs: **Home**, **Exclusions**, **Stats**, **Support**, and **Settings**.

## Using VPN for selected websites and apps

By default, AdGuard VPN for Windows works everywhere. You can add websites and apps you want to exclude from the VPN tunnel to the list of exclusions, or make AdGuard VPN work only on the websites and apps specified in the list of exclusions.

- [Exclusions](/adguard-vpn-for-windows/features/exclusions/)

## App settings

In *App settings*, you can set the app language, enable Kill Switch, auto-update, launch at Windows startup, auto-connect, anonymous app usage data collection, and change the theme. There you can also configure DNS servers and advanced settings.

## Statistics

The Statistics screen shows detailed information about your data usage, categorized by both location and app, for different time periods: daily, monthly, or for the entire time you have been using AdGuard VPN for Windows. The information is stored only on your device and is available to AdGuard VPN subscribers.
