:::info

This article covers the AdGuard VPN browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, Opera, and Edge.

:::

AdGuard VPN browser extension is available for Chrome, Firefox, Opera, and Edge. The UI and functionality doesn’t differ between browsers, so the overview below will be relevant for AdGuard VPN extensions for all browsers.

:::note

You can only use AdGuard VPN Browser Extension when logged in. Sign in with your AdGuard account or via Google, Facebook, or Apple. In the latter case make sure that your account is bound to the same email address as your AdGuard account. If you have an [AdGuard VPN subscription](/general/subscription), it will be activated automatically in AdGuard VPN Browser Extension. Don’t have an AdGuard account yet? [Create it here](https://auth.adguardaccount.com/registration.html).

:::

## Starting with the app

Install the extension from your browser’s extension store and sign in. On the main screen, choose a location and click *Connect*.

- [Installation and removal](/adguard-vpn-browser-extension/installation/)
- [Saved locations](/adguard-vpn-browser-extension/features/saved-locations/)

## Using VPN for selected websites and apps

By default, AdGuard VPN works on all websites, but you can add the ones you want to exclude from the VPN tunnel to the exclusions list. Or you can make AdGuard VPN work only on the websites specified in the list.

- [Exclusions](/adguard-vpn-browser-extension/features/exclusions/)

## Settings

In *Settings*, you can choose a DNS server, set up the connection location for the *Connect* button, and block WebRTC. *General settings* apply to the entire extension: theme, language, context menu, and crash reports.

- [Settings](/adguard-vpn-browser-extension/features/settings/)
- [General settings](/adguard-vpn-browser-extension/features/general-settings/)

## Profiles

You can create up to 10 profiles, each with its own exclusions, DNS server, connection location, and WebRTC blocking settings.

- [Manage profiles](/adguard-vpn-browser-extension/features/profiles/)

## Statistics

The *Stats* section is a simple way to keep track of how much data passes through your VPN connection. All statistics are stored locally on your device.

- [Statistics](/adguard-vpn-browser-extension/features/statistics/)

Known issues and possible solutions.

## Other tabs

The **Account** tab contains only the email address you used to sign into your account and two buttons, *Manage* and *Sign out*. Clicking the *Manage* button takes you to your personal AdGuard account, where you can see all your current licenses and subscriptions, as well as devices connected to them.

The **About** tab is where you can check the extension’s version, visit the official website, and view the EULA or Privacy policy.
